How it's scored

no all-or-nothing

Flow Score blends how often your habits get verified with your recovery rate — how fast you return after a miss. A rough week doesn't erase a good month.

flow_score = 0.7 × verified_rate + 0.3 × recovery_rate

Under the hood

evidence model

A habit isn't a boolean. Every completion carries what was observed and how sure Flow is — so a verified habit can be audited, and a shaky one can be re-checked.

habit_status: verified confidence: 0.94 evidence: [ "session_duration", "active_window", "minimal_phone_activity" ] verified_by: "laptop + phone"

What's real, what's simulated

honesty

Judges deserve the straight answer, so:

Real — the detection engine, evidence scoring, recovery maths, adaptive goals, the AI coach
Real — habit state persists in your browser between visits
Simulated — watch / band / glasses sensor streams and pairing
Not collected — no camera, mic, location or health data is read

Phase 2 swaps the simulated stream for real device APIs. Nothing else in the architecture has to change — that's the point of an evidence engine.
